### 2. Tracking Technologies

To enhance your experience on MobyScreener and deliver relevant functionality, we use a variety of technologies to collect and store data about your interactions with our services. This section outlines the key technologies we employ and how they operate.

**Cookies and Related Technologies**

We and our third-party service providers use cookies and similar technologies to track user activity and preferences across the Service. These include:

* Cookies: Small text files stored on your device that help websites remember user preferences, session information, and login states. These include:
  * Session cookies, which expire once you close your browser.
  * Persistent cookies, which remain on your device for longer periods.
  * First-party cookies, set by MobyScreener.
  * Third-party cookies, set by our analytics, marketing, and functionality partners.
* Local Storage (e.g., HTML5): Browser-based storage mechanisms that offer a cookie-like experience but with greater storage capacity and persistence.
* Web Beacons (Pixel Tags/Clear GIFs): Invisible image files embedded in web pages or emails that allow us to determine whether content was viewed or interacted with. These beacons help us assess the effectiveness of our communications and on-site behavior.
* Software Development Kits (SDKs): Bundles of third-party code integrated into our mobile app that enable us to perform analytics, connect to third-party platforms, and extend functionality. For instance, SDKs may allow social logins, error tracking, or user behavior analysis.
* On-Device Measurement Technologies (ODM): Tools that may locally analyze app and device data to create anonymized usage metrics, depending on your region. For example, we may use Google’s ODM services, which process data on-device and transmit anonymized insights. For more information, see Google’s [Partner Site Policies](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).
* Chat and AI Interaction Tools: Our live chat and AI assistant tools (e.g., Intercom) may utilize cookies and embedded code to track how you engage with support features. This includes storing your chat history, IP address, city/state, and any other information you provide. These tools enable real-time communication and personalization of support.

**Third-Party Access & Data Sharing**

Some of the above technologies are operated by third parties, who may independently collect data about your interactions with the Service over time and across different websites and services. These third parties may use this data in accordance with their own privacy policies.

**Your Choices**

To manage how cookies and similar technologies are used, please review your browser or device settings. Some features of the Service may not function properly if you disable cookies or certain SDK functionality. For more on your rights and how to control tracking technologies, see the Your Choices section of this Privacy Policy.
